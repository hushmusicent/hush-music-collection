💰 Where to sell — fastest route

  • Comics — key / graded books: eBay auction, or Heritage / MyComicShop for high value. Get the good ones graded by CGC first — graded sells for way more.
  • Comics — bulk commons: local shop or convention dealer, sell as a lot. Don't list commons one by one.
  • Cards — Pokémon / Magic: TCGplayer for singles, COMC for bulk. eBay + Whatnot work for most cards.
  • Cards — the hits: PSA grading for the big ones, then eBay auction.
  • Toys / figures: eBay Sold first for the real price, then Mercari or Whatnot. Complete-in-box sells for way more — note if the box / accessories are there.
  • Price everything off SOLD listings, not what people are asking. If it hasn't sold at that price, it isn't worth that price.
  • First 10 to catalog: your 10 most valuable-looking items. Photo the front + back, note the grade, then check sold prices — that's your real collection value.

📸 How Snap & Price works

Take a photo, tap 🔍 Identify with Lens — it shares the photo to Lens on your phone, Lens names it — type the name, then tap each site and type in what it sold for. The app averages those sold prices — that is your real value, not an asking price. Then pick: 📚 Put in my collection to keep it (shows as KEEP, counts in your total value), or 💰 List it for sale to take bids on it. You can flip a kept item to For sale later from Edit.

Why it works this way: no website lets another app pull its live sold prices straight in without a paid account / API key. Lens names it from the photo, the sites show the sold prices, and this app puts them together in one place.

🔨 How bidding works here

Set an item to For sale, add a starting bid and a reserve. Anyone you show the app to can place a bid — the top bid shows live, and it tells you if the reserve is met. When it sells, tap Mark sold and the sold total updates. Tap any Sell / price check button on an item and it opens that site with the item already searched — eBay Sold shows what it actually sold for. Bidding and payment happen on that site, not in this app.
